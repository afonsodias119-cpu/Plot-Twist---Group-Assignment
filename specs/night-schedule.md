> **Update (specs/computer-lab-backup.md):** the group chat, its flags, the message cards and "The fifth element" are gone. Computer 12 saves a backup of the original conversations; Leonor's choice decides the three story endings; Duarte catches a player at computer 12 before the backup.

> **Update (specs/calendar-code.md):** the cover photo is now dated Saturday 17 October, so the code of computer 12 is `1710`, the photo's stamp says 17.10 and the slip on the shutter says 17 October. The clerk's `order` line no longer says the date.

Extends specs/harder-campus.md, specs/evidence-and-hearing.md, specs/pen-and-code.md, specs/campus-people-and-atmosphere.md and specs/continuous-conversations.md. The countdown stays 30 minutes, starts at 22:15 and still pauses while the game is closed.

## Why
The night has a countdown but nothing happens in it: everybody waits in their room until the player arrives, in any order, at any time. Thirty minutes should not be enough for everything. The player should have to choose who to see first, and a slow night should cost something real: a person who has gone home, a card missing at the hearing.

## What
1. **The campus minute.** The night's minute goes from 0 (22:15) to 30 (22:45). It is a whole number or a whole number and a half (12.5 is 22:27 and 30 seconds): only the copy costs half a minute (point 2). It never goes down during a night; a confirmed restart deletes the row, so a new night starts at 0. It grows in two ways: the server charges an accepted action, and the page reports its own minute (point 3).
   - **How the table holds the half.** `nights.minute` keeps the whole minutes, as today: the column is `INTEGER` (specs/evidence-and-hearing.md, point 1) and `db.load_night` turns any other value into 0 (`db.py:117`), so `db.py` is not changed. The half is the note `half`: `campus_evidence.write_night` saves `int(minute)` in the column and `"half": true` or `false` in the notes, and `campus_evidence.read_night` adds 0.5 when the note is exactly `true` and the whole minute is below 30. Cleaning: a column that cannot be read counts as 0 (a hand-written `12.5` included, as `db.py` already does), a whole number outside 0 to 30 is brought back inside, and any other value of `half` counts as no half. So the cleaned minute is always a whole number or a number with a half from 0 to 30.
   - **Every rule compares as before.** Every mark of the schedule is a whole minute (the departures, the rounds, the warnings, `MARKS`, 30), so a half changes no rule: a person leaves when the minute is at or above 10, and at 9.5 the clerk is still there; a round is reached when the old minute is below it and the new one at or above it (11.5 to 12 reaches 12; 12 to 12.5 does not). A rule written "from minute a to minute b" or "in the minute before a round" takes the whole minute (`int(minute)`), so the message `shop` is still due at 9.5 and Duarte is at the lab door at 12.5. The shown time (`night.time`, the header) uses the whole minutes: 12.5 shows `22:27`.

3. **New operation `time`** with exactly `operation`, `player`, `language`, `minute`. `minute` must be an `int` (not a `bool`) from 0 to 30: the page reports whole minutes only, so `5.5` is refused like `5.0`; anything else, a missing field or an extra one is a `ValueError` and nothing is written. When a reply has `fresh` true and `night.minute` 0, the server is starting a new night: the page first sets its time left to 30 minutes, then applies the rule above (after a restart of the host the browser would otherwise push its old time onto the new night). The automatic `talk` after a story action is not a command: no `time` report is sent before it. It never polls. The header shows the campus time before the countdown (`22:27 · ⏳ 17:04`). ## Limits
- The server learns the real time only from the page. A hand-made client that never sends `time` pays only the charged minutes: it skips the waiting, the rude answers and the wrong guesses. It cannot undo a charge, bring a person back, or get a lost clue. A client that sends `time 30` only hurts itself.
- Between two marks the server's minute can be several minutes behind the page's (at most 5: from 0 to 5, from 5 to 10 and from 20 to 25); nothing of the schedule happens between marks.
- A fresh game at minute 0 gives the page 30 minutes again, so a reload before the first report gives back the minutes spent doing nothing (less than 5).

## Out of scope
