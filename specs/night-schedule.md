# A campus that does not wait: a clock the server knows, two people who leave, and Duarte's rounds

Extends specs/harder-campus.md, specs/evidence-and-hearing.md, specs/pen-and-code.md, specs/campus-people-and-atmosphere.md and specs/continuous-conversations.md. It reverses one line of specs/harder-campus.md ("Making the server know the time" was out of scope): from now on the server knows the campus minute. No change to `story.py`, `logic.py`, `db.py`, `game.py`, `app.py` or any reviewed test; the table `nights` already exists. The countdown stays 30 minutes, starts at 22:15 and still pauses while the game is closed.

## Why
The night has a countdown but nothing happens in it: everybody waits in their room until the player arrives, in any order, at any time. Thirty minutes should not be enough for everything. The player should have to choose who to see first, and a slow night should cost something real: a person who has gone home, a card missing at the hearing.

## What
1. **The campus minute.** `nights.minute` is a whole number from 0 (22:15) to 30 (22:45). It never goes down during a night; a confirmed restart deletes the row, so a new night starts at 0. It grows in two ways: the server charges an accepted action, and the page reports its own minute (point 3). A row that cannot be read counts as 0, and a number outside 0 to 30 is brought back inside.
2. **One cost table**, in the new module `campus_clock.py` (rules and numbers; its only text is the shutter sentence of point 5):
   ```python
   COSTS = {"request_copy": 1, "ask_for_help": 1, "accuse": 1, "view_files": 1,   # an accepted story action
            "card_right": 0, "card_wrong": 2, "card_duarte": 1, "caught": 3}       # campus minutes, charged by the server
   PAGE_COSTS = {"rude_answer": 5, "wrong_spot": 1, "wrong_code": 1}               # taken by the page, as today
   NIGHT_MINUTES = 30
   LEAVES = {"amelia": 10, "leonor": 15}       # 22:25 and 22:30
   ROUNDS = (12, 19, 26)                       # 22:27, 22:34, 22:41
   MARKS = (5, 10, 11, 12, 13, 15, 18, 19, 20, 25, 26, 27, 30)   # the minutes the page must report
   ```
   Everything that is not in `COSTS` is free on the server: walking (by a door or by a button), talking, the right place, opening and closing the chat, flagging a message, answering a claim. Reading is never punished; it takes the real time it takes. The six rude answers keep their 5 minutes and the wrong place and wrong code their 1, taken by the page as today (`talk` and the two wrong guesses must keep writing nothing) and reported with `time`. One helper charges after each accepted `game.play` of `campus_game.py` (so a right code and a direct `view_files` cost the same), and `confront` charges its card. A refused action costs nothing.
3. **New operation `time`** with exactly `operation`, `player`, `language`, `minute`. `minute` must be an `int` (not a `bool`) from 0 to 30; anything else, a missing field or an extra one is a `ValueError` and nothing is written. The server keeps the larger of the saved minute and the reported one. A minute that is not larger, or any report after an ending, changes nothing and writes nothing. Reply: the snapshot.
4. **How the page follows.** It keeps drawing its own countdown. After every reply that is a snapshot (a `talk` reply has no `night`, and the start menu asked for again after a story action is one) it takes `night.charged` minutes off its time, once, and then sets its time left to the smaller of its own and 30 minutes minus `night.minute`, so a cleared browser or another device no longer gets 30 new minutes. When a reply has `fresh` true and `night.minute` 0, the server is starting a new night: the page first sets its time left to 30 minutes, then applies the rule above (after a restart of the host the browser would otherwise push its old time onto the new night). It sends `time` with its own whole minute: before a command, when it is ahead of `night.minute`; when it reaches `night.report_at`; and when the time is up. A command that was waiting for a `time` report is not sent when the reply to that report is a catch (it is not accepted), has `night.closed`, or no longer has in `people` the person the command is for (the person of the open card, or the person just clicked). The automatic `talk` after a story action is not a command: no `time` report is sent before it. It never polls. The header shows the campus time before the countdown (`22:27 · ⏳ 17:04`). The page no longer takes `confront.minutes` off by itself: the same minutes arrive in `night.charged`. `const COUNTDOWN_MINUTES = 30`, the `const running = ...` line and `if (option.cost) penalize(option.cost)` are not touched.
5. **Two people leave.** The clerk is at the print shop until minute 10 (22:25. Her first line, "We close in ten minutes.", is exact only for a player who reaches her at 22:15; the time the player can rely on is the one in the Clues app and in the message `shop`). Leonor is in the library until minute 15 (22:30), and she leaves by the schedule only while she is undecided. Once she is helping she stays, saving her screenshots. After `accuse` the story has already emptied her table and nothing changes at minute 15: the library keeps the story's paragraph, `night.gone` does not list her, and her actions get the story's own answer. Once a person has left by the schedule the room can still be entered, but the person is not in `people`, `talk` and `confront` refuse as for anyone who is not there, and the story action (`request_copy`, also through `assemble`, specs/torn-photo.md; `ask_for_help` and `accuse`) is refused with a schedule sentence and nothing is saved. The schedule is checked before the story's own rules: once the person has left, and while the saved scene is in that person's room and the game has not ended, the action gets the schedule sentence whatever the story would have answered there. From another room, and after an ending, the story answers as today (`not_here`, `game_over`).
   - The two sentences: "The shutter is down and the counter is dark. The clerk went home at 22:25. A slip is taped to the shutter: cover photo, order of 14 November, collect tomorrow." / "Leonor's chair is empty. Her book is still open at the same page." The slip has no quotation marks, so it is not a speech bubble. It gives the date of the code a second source: until now the clerk's line `order` was the only one. ## Reviewed tests that change
None. With no report the minute is the sum of the charges, and the reviewed paths charge at most 3 minutes in a night (the copy, the decision, the files, each once): measured over the 201 existing tests with a throwaway plugin that only counts these charges, the counter is used at minute 0, Leonor at minute 1 and the chat at minute 1 at the latest, and no test passes minute 3, so none meets minute 10, 12 or 15. The tests of tests/test_evidence_and_hearing.py stay unchanged too: `confront.minutes` keeps its values (the server now adds them to the minute instead of the page taking them off), no test there asserts that a rebuff saves nothing, and none charges more than 6 minutes in one game.

## Limits
- The server learns the real time only from the page. A hand-made client that never sends `time` pays only the charged minutes: it skips the waiting, the rude answers and the wrong guesses. It cannot undo a charge, bring a person back, or get a lost clue. A client that sends `time 30` only hurts itself.
- Between two marks the server's minute can be several minutes behind the page's (at most 5: from 0 to 5, from 5 to 10 and from 20 to 25); nothing of the schedule happens between marks.
- A fresh game at minute 0 gives the page 30 minutes again, so a reload before the first report gives back the minutes spent doing nothing (less than 5).
- `present_evidence`, `open_drawer` and `view_files` played directly are still accepted, as in specs/pen-and-code.md.
- The balance is a model of people, not a play test: 15 to 60 seconds a command are assumptions. The model has no knowledge either: a route types the right code whenever it wants, so each row says what its player knows. The numbers are in one small file so they can be retuned, and the tests below fail if a retune breaks a claim.
- The model starts in the study room. The 3D game starts on the plaza: the walk to the study room is one more door (15 to 60 seconds) that no route counts. With it, `fifth_element` with every clue no longer fits at 60 seconds (the lab-only route still does, at 22.1), the careful first night at 45 seconds keeps 2.7 minutes and the best night 2.1.
- A first night can lose the photo and Leonor by minute 10. That is intended (the closing times are in the Clues app from the start, the date of the code stays on the shutter, and the night still ends), but it is harsh. So is a catch at 22:27 for a player who has not yet seen Leonor.
- Duarte's seat in the auditorium is never empty for the rules: the picture at the lab door only illustrates.
- The public host forgets the night with the saves when it restarts.

## Out of scope
A night longer or shorter than 30 minutes, charging for talking or walking, closing a door (the print shop can always be entered), Duarte walking, new people, sounds for the rounds, a schedule in the plain game. If time is short, cut in this order and keep the rest: Duarte at the lab door in 3D, the shutter picture. Never cut the lost-clue rule.

For an old operation, which refuses extra fields already today, the test first checks that a valid `open` returns `night`.
