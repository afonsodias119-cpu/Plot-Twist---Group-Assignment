# Evidence and the hearing: show people what you found, and answer Duarte's three claims

Extends specs/campus-people-and-atmosphere.md, specs/chat-dialogues.md, specs/harder-campus.md, specs/pen-and-code.md and specs/continuous-conversations.md. No change to `story.py`, `logic.py`, `game.py`, `app.py` or any reviewed test: no new flag, action or ending, so every saved game stays valid and the plain game plays as before. `db.py` gains one table and three small functions. The existing operations keep their exact fields, and `talk` still writes nothing.

## Why
The player collects proof and never uses it. Apart from two fixed answers (Tomás and Leonor can be shown the photo), nothing the player holds can be put in front of a person, and the whole case ends with one click on "Present the evidence": the game cannot tell a player who understood the lie from one who walked through the rooms. Evidence should be something you put in front of a person, and the auditorium should be the place where Duarte fights back.

## What
1. **A night's notes, kept by the server.** `db.init_db` also creates the table `nights` (`player TEXT PRIMARY KEY`, `minute INTEGER NOT NULL`, `notes TEXT NOT NULL`, a JSON object), beside `games` and in the same call, so no read ever creates a table (reviewed tests compare the database file byte for byte). New in `db.py`: `load_night(player)` (returns `{"minute": 0, "notes": {}}` when there is no row or it cannot be read; it never writes), `save_night(player, minute, notes)` and `delete_night(player)`. Example row: `("ana", 0, '{"cracked": ["tomas"], "hearing": ["files", "concede"], "verdict": ""}')`. `minute` stays 0 in this feature (specs/night-schedule.md uses it). A confirmed `restart`, and an `open` that starts a new game, delete the row. The save itself cannot hold these notes: a save must have exactly the story's flags (`logic.py:138`) and a restart replaces it (`game.py:89`).
2. **What is read is cleaned.** `campus_evidence.read_night(player, state)` keeps only what it understands: `cracked` is a list of people who have a confrontation (others are dropped); `hearing` is absent or a list of at most three answers, each `"concede"` or a card id, and only while the saved scene is the auditorium or the game has ended; `verdict` is kept only when the game has ended and the caps of point 10 could have produced it. Anything else counts as empty. A damaged row can neither raise an error nor help the player. Cleaning happens in memory; nothing is written until the next operation that saves.
3. **The case file, on the server only.** New module `campus_case.py` (data only, like `campus_search.py`; never imported by `page3d.py`): `EVIDENCE` (card id and the story condition that holds it, checked with `logic._condition_met`), `FACTS`, `CONFRONT`, `HEARING` and `CASE_TEXT` (labels, reveals, rebuffs, claims, the professor's lines and the verdict lines, in `en` and `pt` with the same keys). Short example:
   ```python
   EVIDENCE = {"original_photo": {"has": ["original_photo"]}, "files": {"flags": {"files_viewed": True}},
               "leonor_messages": {"flags": {"colleague_choice": "help"}}, "msg_5": {"flags": {"flagged_5": True}}}   # and msg_1 to msg_4
   FACTS = {"tomas": "tomas_word", "rita": "rita_word"}
   CONFRONT = {"tomas": {"node": "slides", "breaks": ["msg_5"]}, "rita": {"node": "five", "breaks": ["files", "original_photo"]},
               "leonor": {"node": "sure", "breaks": ["tomas_word"]}}
   HEARING = [{"id": "name", "accepts": ["files"], "wrong": {}},
              {"id": "photo", "accepts": ["original_photo", "rita_word"], "wrong": {"files": "photo_files"}},
              {"id": "messages", "accepts": ["leonor_messages", "tomas_word"], "wrong": {"msg_1": "messages_flag"}}]   # and msg_2 to msg_5
   ```
   `wrong` names the professor's line for a wrong card that has a line of its own; every other wrong card gets the general wrong line of that claim. New module `campus_evidence.py` holds the rules that read it (`read_night`, `held`, `confront`, `hearing`, `hearing_view`, `shown_ending`).
4. **Evidence cards.** The server decides what the player holds, from the save plus the night's notes, and every snapshot carries it as `evidence`: a list of `{"id", "label"}` with only the cards held now, in this order. Ten ids: `original_photo` (the item), `files` (`files_viewed`), `leonor_messages` (Leonor's screenshots, while `colleague_choice` is `help`), `msg_1` to `msg_5` (each flagged chat message; the label is `🚩` and the story's own text of that message) and the two facts won by cracking people, `tomas_word` and `rita_word`. The diary lines stay hints: the server never sees them.
5. **"📁 Show evidence" in every conversation with a person.** Not at computer 12 and not during the hearing. The button (`evidence_show`) is on every line of every person except a `closed` line, lie or not, so the player has to notice the lie. It lives in its own row of the conversation card (`id="talk-evidence"`, between the log and the answers), never inside `#talk-options`: tests/continuous-conversations.cjs reads the children of `#talk-options` as the list of answers. Pressing it replaces the answers with the cards held (the technique of `askCode`); the log, `topics` and `asked` are not touched. Choosing a card adds a bubble on the right and sends `confront` with the line on the screen. `evidence_back` puts back the same buttons that were there (the page keeps them aside): nothing is sent and nothing is added to the log. The line on the screen is the last line the server gave by `talk` (the page stores it in `talkStep` and in the refresh after a story action, where its node is `start`). A rebuff does not change it, so a second card can be tried on the same line; after a reply with `cracked` true it becomes `start`. The row is part of the conversation card, so the text mode has it too.
6. **New operation `confront`** with exactly `operation`, `player`, `language`, `npc`, `node`, `evidence`.
   - Refused with `ValueError`, nothing written: `npc` is not a text, is unknown or is computer 12; `node` is unknown or is a line `talk` would not give now (not reachable yet, or a claim already broken); the person is not in the saved room; the game has ended; `evidence` is not a text or not one of the ten ids; a card the player does not hold; a hearing is open; missing or extra fields.
   - The right card on the claim line of a person not yet cracked: the person is added to `cracked` (the only write), and their fact, if they have one, is a card from now on.
   - Any other card, or any other line: nothing changes.
   - Reply: the snapshot plus `"confront": {"npc", "name", "cracked", "text", "trust", "note", "minutes"}`. `text` is the reveal or the rebuff; `trust` is 3 for a reveal of Tomás or Leonor and null for Rita's reveal, for a rebuff and for Duarte's line (the trust stays as it was); `note` is a diary line (empty for a rebuff); `minutes` is 0 for the right card, 2 for a rebuff and 1 for any card shown to Duarte. In this feature the page takes `minutes` off its countdown exactly as it does for a rude answer (`penalize`); specs/night-schedule.md moves the charge to the server. The reply never says which card would have been right.
   - The page draws the reply as a line of that person with no node: `text`, `trust`, `note`, the answers the line on the screen had (`showLine` adds the kept topics and hides the asked ones), and the story actions of the snapshot whose `person` is that person.
7. **Three confrontations** (every line is in the dialogue script; the specs name them by id).
   - Tomás, line `slides` (new, from `kind`: "Ask who made the slides", offered like `msg5` only once the files are viewed): "the chat says Duarte did them, under my name. So maybe he did, and I only tidied up." Broken only by `msg_5`, the flagged message with his name. He reads its arrival line: "At 11:49 I was sitting right here, one computer away from the one in the hood. I did not type a word." Gives `tomas_word`.
   - Rita, line `five` (new, from `photo`: "Ask why the photo in the folder shows five"): "Then I counted wrong. A photo cannot change. My memory can." Broken by `files` or `original_photo`. Gives `rita_word`.
   - Leonor, her existing line `sure`, broken by `tomas_word`: trust 3 and a diary line, no fact. It is optional; her two story decisions are unchanged.
   - Once a claim is broken no answer that leads to its line is offered again, and the line cannot be asked for. On the page: the claim line was chosen in this conversation, so its id is in `asked` and its kept answer is not drawn again; when a `confront` reply has `cracked` true the page also deletes that id from `topics`. Closing the card empties both lists, and the server's menus no longer have the answer. The page never asks for a broken line: the `ValueError` is only for hand-made requests.
   - Everybody else has one rebuff line; Duarte has one line for any card ("Keep it for the professor.").
   - Each teammate vouches only for their own chat message: Tomás for the one with his name, Rita for "four of us, finally", Leonor (new line `mine`, offered only once she is helping) for the one about the study room. Nobody lists the fake messages.
8. **Dialogue changes in `campus_people.py`**, both languages, same ids and conditions: new lines `slides` (Tomás), `five` (Rita), `mine` (Leonor) and the three answers that lead to them; Tomás's existing line `msg5` is reworded (it is under his name and he does not remember writing it) so that it never contradicts `slides`. Its id, condition and trust stay and it still has a note, reworded to match ("Tomás does not remember writing message 5; it reached his phone last night." / "O Tomás não se lembra de ter escrito a mensagem 5; chegou-lhe ao telemóvel ontem à noite."). No new answer has a `cost`. Rita's `photo` feeds the kept topics like Tomás's `kind` (one more condition on the line at `play.js:591`: `line.npc === "rita" && line.node === "photo"`), so `five` stays on offer after another subject. The teammate's test still passes: its `photo` line has only Goodbye. Tomás's `kind` and Leonor's `start` (while she is helping) now have four answers of their own, and Rita's `photo` gains one. With the kept topics a card also shows the answers kept from the start menu: Tomás's `kind` can show six (`who`, `password`, `msg5`, `slides`, and the kept `hard` and `photo`).
9. **The hearing replaces the one-click presentation.** On the 3D page the professor's "Present the evidence to the professor" (drawn like any other answer, specs/actions-when-they-make-sense.md, with the line `hearing_hint` under it) starts the hearing instead of playing `present_evidence`; the page hides the story button with one line beside the `open_drawer` one (`play.js:627`), and the server still offers and accepts it. The hearing button does not use `talkAction` (which plays the action and asks for the start menu again): it sends `hearing` with `start`. The hearing is played inside the professor's conversation card: the server sends the log as entries with `who`, so each bubble has the right name and voice, and the doubt is shown in words under the claim number (`hearing_trust`: three minus the doubts, out of 3; conversations have no hearts since specs/cleaner-interface.md). The answers are the cards held and "Say nothing". `showHearing` draws the card itself and never calls `showLine`: at the start it empties the log, `topics` and `asked`, hides the evidence row and the story actions, and then draws `hearing.said` and the cards. There is no new overlay: the card is the same in the text mode. While the snapshot has a `hearing` and no ending, talking to the professor (button, note or K) calls `showHearing` with it instead of sending `talk`, so a closed card or a reloaded page shows the hearing where it stopped.
10. **New operation `hearing`** with exactly `operation`, `player`, `language`, `answer`; `answer` is `"start"`, `"concede"` or a card id.
    - `ValueError`, nothing written: `answer` is not a text, is not one of those, or is a card the player does not hold; missing or extra fields.
    - Refused with `accepted: false`, nothing written: the saved scene is not the auditorium, or an answer comes before `"start"` (message: the story's `not_here`); the game has ended, so a fourth answer is refused (the story's `game_over`); `"start"` when the hearing has started (`hearing_locked`).
    - Duarte makes three claims, in this order: `name` (broken by `files`), `photo` (he is "the one on the right, in the long coat"; broken by `original_photo` or `rita_word`) and `messages` (broken by `leonor_messages` or `tomas_word`). A flagged message alone does not break `messages`, and the professor says why: "You flagged it. That is your opinion of it." `files` on `photo` has its own line too: the history says a photo was swapped, it does not show the one before.
    - One answer per claim, no retry. A card that does not break the claim is a doubt; `"concede"` is always a doubt. The doubts are never counted above three (specs/night-schedule.md can start a player at one): with three, a further wrong answer changes nothing and the professor's trust is 0.
    - Each accepted answer is saved in the notes (`hearing`), so closing the card or reloading cannot take a doubt back.
    - After the third answer the server itself plays the story's `present_evidence`, so the saved ending is still "the first ending whose condition is true". Then the verdict caps what is shown: three doubts or more show `stolen_credits` whatever the story's ending was; two doubts show `impostor` when the story's ending is `full_case`; otherwise the story's ending is shown. The shown id is saved as `verdict`.
    - Reply: the snapshot plus `"heard"` (true when the answer was saved). From `"start"` on, also after the ending, the snapshot has `hearing` = `{step, doubts, said}`, rebuilt from the saved answers; it is `None` only when no hearing was started tonight. `said` always ends with the claim that is waiting for an answer (after `start`: claim 1; after one answer: claim 1, the player's card, the professor's line, claim 2); after the third answer it ends with the reply to that answer (the professor's line, or Duarte's line after `concede`) and `step` stays 3. Example after one wrong answer: `{"step": 2, "doubts": 1, "said": [{"who": "duarte", "name": "Duarte", "text": "..."}, {"who": "you", "name": "", "text": "📁 ..."}, {"who": "professor", "name": "Professor", "text": "..."}, {"who": "duarte", "name": "Duarte", "text": "..."}]}`.
11. **The ending that is shown.** The snapshot's `ending` is the verdict's ending (id, title and text of that story ending) with one more field, `verdict`: a line of the professor shown above the text when a cap applied (three doubts; three doubts with Leonor helping: "The professor closes the list before Leonor reaches the door."; two doubts on a full case), or when `stolen_credits` follows a broken `messages` claim (`no_originals`); empty otherwise. The verdict is never joined to `ending.text`. The page shows it above the text. The reply to the third answer must not be lost either: `render` closes the conversation card as soon as an ending arrives (`play.js:433`), and `showHearing` draws only while there is no ending. So the ending card also shows the last entry of `hearing.said` (the reply to the third answer) above the verdict line, with its speaker's name. With voices on the page speaks the quote of that reply first, in the voice of its `who`, then the quote of the verdict line in the professor's voice, then the ending's quotes with `ending_voices` as today. An ending reached with no hearing has neither line. The row in `games` keeps the story's own ending. The collection of endings and the share card use the shown ending.
12. **Nothing else during the hearing.** From `"start"` until the ending, `play`, `enter`, `search`, `unlock` and `assemble` (specs/torn-photo.md) are refused with `hearing_locked` ("You cannot walk out in the middle of your own case.") and `talk` and `confront` raise it; `open`, `restart` and `hearing` work.
13. **Files.** `app_3d.py`: `CODE_MODULES` becomes `story`, `logic`, `db`, `game`, `campus_text`, `campus_people`, `campus_search`, `campus_case`, `campus_evidence`, `campus_game`, `page3d` (a module after the ones it imports from); neither new module goes in `PAGE_FILES`. `campus_game.py`: the two operations in `allowed_fields` (`campus_game.py:231`), `evidence` and `hearing` in the snapshot, the answers to broken claims taken out of a `talk` reply (reading the notes writes nothing), the row wiped on restart. `play.js` and `play.css`: the tray, the hearing, the reply to the third answer and the verdict line on the ending card; new lines beside the pinned ones, none of them edited. The lines from `function showLine(line) {` to the comment `// The player chose to do something` are run alone by tests/continuous-conversations.cjs with a fake page: new code there calls a new function only inside a click handler, reads `current.evidence`, `current.hearing`, `current.night` and `current.memory` as possibly missing, and both marker lines stay as they are. The evidence row is drawn inside `showLine` on every line, so it runs in that test: on elements it uses only what the fake page has (tests/continuous-conversations.cjs:9-13: plain properties such as `textContent` and `hidden`, `dataset`, `classList.toggle`, `append`, `replaceChildren`, `setAttribute`, `addEventListener`; not `classList.add` and not `remove()`), and it never indexes a text of `ui()`, which is `{}` there (`t.evidence_show` is fine, `t.night_lines[0]` breaks). ## Limits
- The hearing and the tray are puzzles, not security. The server keeps accepting `present_evidence`, `open_drawer` and `view_files` played directly, because reviewed tests play them: a hand-made request can skip the hearing before it starts. That path gives the story's own ending, never a cap.
- In this feature the minutes of a rebuff are taken in the browser, like the rude answers: a hand-made request can show cards without losing time. It still cannot show a card the player does not hold, and no reply says which card is right.
- The shown ending can differ from the ending saved in `games` (the caps). The story decides the best the player earned; the hearing can only lose it. The plain game is not affected.
- The notes live in the same database file as the saves, so the public host forgets both when it restarts. A name is not a password: whoever types it continues that night.
- Tomás's promise to stand in the door has no effect until specs/night-schedule.md.
- The page code is checked only by needles in its source and by the teammate's JavaScript test; the tray and the hearing need the browser check, in 3D and in text mode.

## Out of scope
The clerk's reprint slip, any new story flag, action or ending, a card or overlay of its own for the hearing, lights or camera for the hearing in 3D, answering a claim twice, a server clock (specs/night-schedule.md), memory of earlier nights and a fifth ending (specs/earlier-nights.md), the plain game. If time is short, cut Leonor's optional chain at `sure` and the `mine` line first. Never cut the caps of point 10 or the saving of the hearing in the notes.
