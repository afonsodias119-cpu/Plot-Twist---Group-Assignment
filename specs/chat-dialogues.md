# Chat-style conversations and room plaques

Extends specs/campus-people-and-atmosphere.md. Only what is on the screen and the dialogue data change: no change to `story.py`, `logic.py`, `db.py`, `game.py`, `app.py`, the server rules or any reviewed test.

## Why
The conversations show only the last line and a list of buttons, and every answer offers "Ask something else", so talking feels like a menu, not a conversation. The room names float as round amber pills with an emoji, which does not look like a building.

## What
1. **Conversations look like a messaging app.** The conversation card keeps the whole conversation as message bubbles: what the person says on the left, what the player chose on the right, each with the time of the game clock (`state.clock`). While the answer is on its way, a "typing…" bubble is shown. The header has a round avatar with the person's initial, the name and the trust hearts. The answers are reply buttons under the messages. Closing the conversation (or talking to someone else) starts an empty one next time.
2. **No "Ask something else".** Every "Ask something else" / "Perguntar outra coisa" answer is removed from `campus_people.py`, in both languages. Every line still offers at least one answer (a question that goes on, or goodbye), and "End conversation" is always in the header. (Changed by specs/continuous-conversations.md: the conversation now stays open and offers the topics not asked yet, so the player no longer has to talk again to change subject.) The answers that only end the conversation ("Goodbye", "Leave") stay in the data, because every line must offer an answer, but the screen does not show them: "End conversation" in the header is the way out, and it is highlighted when nothing else is left to answer. A question already chosen in the open conversation is not offered again (for example after "Back off" brings the talk back to its start). After an action done in a conversation, the person answers with its result (the "Continue the conversation" answer and its `talk_continue` text are removed); since specs/continuous-conversations.md, the topics not asked yet are offered again after that result.
3. **Only what is said goes in a bubble.** A line is split by its quotation marks (the same rule the voices use, `partsOf` in `play.js`): what is between quotation marks is a speech bubble of the person; the rest (what the person does, for example "Rita looks up from her laptop.") is a short narration line in the middle of the conversation, outside the bubbles. A line without quotation marks (computer 12, the result of an action) is only narration. A short quote without a full stop (three words or fewer, for example someone added "Duarte" to the report) is a name, not a line someone says: it stays in the narration.
4. **No scene description in the side panel.** In the 3D game the panel shows the room's title, the people and the actions, but not the description of the scene: the player has to look around and talk. The text mode (when the 3D cannot load) keeps the description, because there it is the only way to know where you are.
5. **Room plaques.** The room names over the doors are plaques like the signs of a real building: only the name in capitals, a thin amber edge, no emoji and no number. The names of the people are small dark name tags. Computer 12 has no label: its green light and the "press K" note show it.
6. **Only the people of the group have names.** The print shop clerk is called "Clerk" / "Funcionária" (she was "Amélia"), in the conversation and on her name tag, which follows the language. Her id stays `amelia`, so nothing else changes.
7. **The case file says who is in the group.** The mission box lists the four real members as small name chips ("You", Leonor, Rita, Tomás) and says that the fifth name on the cover, Duarte, was never part of the group (`group_label`, `group`, `group_note` in `page3d.py`, both languages).
8. **The last name is remembered.** After a game opens, its name is kept in this browser and filled in on the start screen next time. `app_3d.py` builds the page again whenever one of its files changes (an update of the app), and the browser then shows the start screen: with the name already there, continuing is one click.

## Out of scope
New dialogue lines, saving conversations, changing who says what, the room card of the visual walkthrough (hidden in the playable game), anything on the server.
