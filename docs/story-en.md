# Story — The Fifth Element (summary in English)

A short English summary of [historia.md](historia.md), the team's reference (in Portuguese). The exact texts the player
reads, in both languages, are in the code: `story.py`, `prototypes/campus-3d/campus_people.py` and `campus_case.py`.
Every Portuguese text has an English twin with the same ids, answers and conditions.

## Premise

The night before the presentation, a fifth person appears in the photo of your group of four. His name, Duarte, is on
the cover of the report, and the group has received messages that seem to prove he always took part. You have the night
(22:30 to 23:00 in the 3D game) to gather the proofs and show them to the professor in the auditorium.

## The four proofs

| Proof | How you get it | Breaks Duarte's claim |
|---|---|---|
| The file history | Find the USB stick on the study room table; open computer 12 with the code (the day of the photo, worked out with the group). | "My name has been on the cover since the first draft." |
| The original photo | Put the torn photo back together with the clerk in the print shop. | "Five people in the photo." |
| The backup of the conversations | Save it at computer 12 after opening the files. | "Weeks of messages in the group." |
| The security recording | With José, the guard: explain the situation, show him a proof, plug in the cables, keep the 23:47 picture of the computer lab. | "I was not even here last night." |

## The ending

The professor offers "📁 Show evidence" or "Ask how many members". Showing evidence lets you select the proofs you have.
With a proof missing, he says only how many are missing and the night goes on. With all four, the case is closed. If
23:00 comes first, the faculty closes and the fifth name stays on the cover.
